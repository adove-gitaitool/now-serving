    What's going on

    A long service sits at the counter for more of the clock, so a random arrival is more likely to land inside one. With exponential service times averaging 10 minutes, the customer being served when you walk in takes about 20 minutes on average, and about 74% of them take longer than 10.

    It's the same effect as in the spreadsheet: 6.90 min for all 25 customers, 10.74 min for the ones found being served.
